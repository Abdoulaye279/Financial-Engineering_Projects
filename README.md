# Financial Engineering

Quant finance projects alongside my engineering physics studies at UM6P. All in Python, data and notebooks included.

- [black-scholes-sp500](black-scholes-sp500): Black-Scholes tested on 26 years of S&P 500 data; its assumptions fail
- [var-es-sp500](var-es-sp500): backtesting VaR / Expected Shortfall models on the S&P 500, then on portfolios (with Apple, then seven assets including gold and bonds)
- [industry-allocation](industry-allocation): estimated risk vs 1/N: minimum variance, risk parity and Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage on 30 US industries, 1931-2018
- [market-signals](market-signals): direction, regimes (hidden Markov model) and pairs on the S&P 500, each against the right baseline
- [lehman-ai-agents](lehman-ai-agents): asking an LLM to find the warning signs in Lehman Brothers' last 10-Q before the bankruptcy

Every notebook has an "Open in Colab" badge at the top, so nothing needs to be installed.
